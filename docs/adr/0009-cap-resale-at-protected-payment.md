# Cap Pass resale at the Protected payment

A Pass resale price is denominated in USDC, must be greater than zero, and cannot exceed the original Protected payment. The listed price is the buyer's final total, with Mint Up's 9% fee deducted from the seller's proceeds rather than added at checkout. This limits scalping and ensures that an Event cancellation never returns less than an eligible marketplace buyer paid, while still allowing a seller to offer a discount.
