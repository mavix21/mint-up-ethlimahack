# Keep public Pass resale inside Mint Up

Public listings require a new Event Pass contract deployment because the existing contract binds every offer to one designated buyer and is not upgradeable. The new contract permits any eligible buyer authorized by Mint Up to purchase an open listing, while standard approvals and transfers remain disabled. A listing becomes public only after verified onchain reconciliation and is invalidated by purchase, withdrawal, Event start or cancellation, check-in, ownership change, or a contract pause; listings invalidated by a pause do not revive. The private-offer flow and pilot Passes are replaced rather than supported alongside the new model. This makes resale publicly discoverable without allowing external marketplaces to bypass Mint Up eligibility, lifecycle, payment, or fee rules.
